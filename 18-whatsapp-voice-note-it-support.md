## WhatsApp / Voice Note IT Support

---

**BEFORE YOU SEND A VOICE NOTE TO THE IT DESK...**

- I cannot troubleshoot a DNS resolution failure from a 47-second voice note recorded in a wind tunnel.
- "The thingy is not working" delivered via voice note at 2 AM is not a support request. It is a noise complaint.
- Text is searchable. Voice notes are not. If you need me to remember what you said next week, type it.

*Note: A voice note that starts with "Can you hear me?" and ends with "Hello?" adds 24 hours to the response time.*

---

**BEFORE YOU TAKE A PHOTO OF YOUR SCREEN WITH YOUR PHONE...**

- A photograph of your monitor taken at a 45-degree angle with potato-quality lighting and visible fingerprints is not a screenshot.
- Screenshots exist. There is a button on your keyboard. It says "PrtScn." Use it. If you do not know where it is, ask Google before you ask me.
- If you must photograph the screen, hold the phone steady, turn off the flash, and do not include your coffee mug, your lunch, or your reflection in the frame.

*Note: A blurry photo of a blurry error message that you immediately closed before I could read it does not help me. At all.*

---

**BEFORE YOU WHATSAPP ME AT 11 PM 'JUST A QUICK QUESTION'...**

- The IT Bible has a verse about this: "Just a quick question is never quick, and it is rarely just a question."
- Texting me after hours for a non-emergency does not make you proactive. It makes you the reason I turn off read receipts.
- If the office is closed and the building is locked, the password reset can wait until morning. The server is not going anywhere.

*Note: My WhatsApp status says "Support hours: 8-5." Your message saying "I know but this is urgent" at 10 PM is a violation of the digital social contract.*

---

**BEFORE YOU SEND A WALL OF 20 SCREENSHOTS WITH NO CONTEXT...**

- Twenty screenshots of different error messages from different applications with no explanation is not a support ticket. It is a digital ransom note.
- Circle the relevant part. Number them. Tell me what order to look at them. I am a technician, not a detective reconstructing your workflow from disjointed visual evidence.
- "See attached" with 47 files named "Screenshot(1).png" through "Screenshot(47).png" is how you get ignored until you provide actual context.

*Note: Screenshots are evidence. Context is the investigation. Provide both or expect no conviction.*

---

**BEFORE YOU ASK ME TO 'JUST FIX IT VIA WHATSAPP'...**

- I cannot reboot your server from my phone's WhatsApp interface. I cannot remote into your machine via text message. I cannot deploy a patch through an image attachment.
- Remote support requires a remote support tool. That requires you to be at your computer. That requires you to have scheduled a session.
- "Can't you just do your IT magic from there?" — no. My "IT magic" requires a VPN connection, your cooperation, and you not walking away mid-session.

*Note: WhatsApp is for quick coordination, not full-scale infrastructure repairs. Call me old-fashioned, but some things require an actual computer.*

---

**BEFORE YOU SEND ME A SCREENSHOT OF A SCREENSHOT...**

- A screenshot of a screenshot that has been compressed, forwarded three times, and received as a low-resolution JPEG is illegible.
- The error message text has been reduced to indistinguishable pixels. The QR code cannot be scanned. The serial number has become abstract art.
- The original screenshot was clear. Why did you not send that one? Why did you take a screenshot of the screenshot and then send the meta-screenshot?

*Note: Screenshot-ception is not impressive. It is infuriating. Send the original or do not bother.*

---

**BEFORE YOU SEND A 'VOICE NOTE' EXPLAINING THE PROBLEM...**

- A voice note longer than 5 minutes about a printer issue is not acceptable in any universe.
- You could have typed the entire problem in 30 seconds. You chose to record a 4-minute rambling monologue with three interruptions, background TV noise, and heavy breathing into the microphone.
- I will transcribe it. Badly. Then I will respond with a single sentence because that is all the actual issue required.

*Note: Voice notes are for telling someone you love them or that you are running late. They are not for explaining a RAID array failure.*
