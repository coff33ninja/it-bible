## The Wrong Department

---

**BEFORE YOU CALL IT BECAUSE THE TOILET IS LEAKING...**

- Sir, this is the IT department. I manage Active Directory, not plumbing. I do not have a plunger. I have a keyboard.
- "But you guys fix everything, right?" — no. We fix everything that plugs into a wall socket and runs electricity. We do not fix what plugs into the sewage line.
- The plumber's number is on the same intranet page as the IT helpdesk number. Scroll down. It is right there.

*Note: Calling IT for a blocked toilet will result in your ticket being assigned to /dev/urinal.*

---

**BEFORE YOU ASK IT TO ORDER YOUR STATIONERY...**

- I manage the servers. I do not manage the supply cupboard. I do not know where the blue pens are.
- "Can you order me a new chair?" — I can order you a new docking station. I cannot order you an ergonomic throne to sit on while you browse Facebook.
- If you need staples, paper clips, or a new mug, call procurement. Their extension is literally one digit off from ours. Yes, we know. No, we will not transfer you for the fifth time.

*Note: IT is not the general office admin department. We do not stock sticky notes.*

---

**BEFORE YOU CALL IT BECAUSE THE AIR CONDITIONER IS BROKEN...**

- The server room AC is IT's responsibility because the servers will melt without it. Your office AC is facilities management's problem.
- I cannot "just tweak the building thermostat from my computer." That is not a thing. I do not have magic climate control software.
- "But it's 30 degrees in here and my laptop is overheating" — then take a break, go outside, and let facilities know. Do not call IT to complain about the weather inside your office.

*Note: The AC being broken is uncomfortable. It is not an IT emergency. Unless the server room is 40 degrees, do not call us.*

---

**BEFORE YOU BRING YOUR PERSONAL LAPTOP TO IT FOR FREE REPAIRS...**

- I support company-issued equipment. Your personal Dell that you bought in 2018 from Cash Converters is not covered by our support contract.
- "Can you just take a quick look?" — no. Because "a quick look" becomes two hours of unpaid work, and then I am responsible for whatever happens next.
- The company IT budget does not cover your son's gaming PC, your wife's MacBook, or your uncle's "computer that won't turn on."

*Note: We do not offer "IT for the whole family" plans. We are not Telkom. We are the corporate IT department.*

---

**BEFORE YOU CALL IT BECAUSE THE OFFICE DOOR WON'T OPEN...**

- The access control system falls under IT's umbrella. The physical door handle does not. If the handle is loose, call maintenance.
- "My access card stopped working" — that is IT. "The door is stuck and I cannot push it open" — that is not IT.
- We do not carry WD-40. We do not carry screwdrivers for hinges. We carry screwdrivers for server chassis. There is a difference.

*Note: If it requires oil, it is not an IT problem. If it requires a reboot, it might be.*

---

**BEFORE YOU CALL IT TO FIX THE COFFEE MACHINE...**

- The coffee machine has a network connection for usage tracking. That network connection is IT's responsibility. The machine itself is not.
- "The coffee machine is not working" — is it plugged in? Is the water reservoir full? Has it been descaled this century? These are not IT questions.
- I do not know how to fix a coffee machine. I know how to fix a server. If I attempt to fix your coffee machine, you will not like the result.

*Note: The coffee machine is not on our SLA. The printer is also not on our SLA, but we fix that anyway because we have given up fighting that battle.*

---

**BEFORE YOU CALL IT BECAUSE THE LIGHTS WON'T TURN ON...**

- Light switches exist. They are on the wall. They are not controlled by our server. We do not have a "lights" dashboard.
- "The fluorescent tube in the ceiling is flickering" — replace the tube. That is not a DNS issue. I promise you.
- If the building's power is out, that is Eskom. If the UPS is beeping, that is IT. If the overhead light is buzzing, that is facilities. Learn the difference.

*Note: The only lights we control are the blinking ones on the network switches. The ceiling lights are not our jurisdiction.*
