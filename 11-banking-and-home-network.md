**BEFORE YOU COMPLAIN THAT YOUR HOME WI-FI IS 'BROKEN AND SLOW'...**

- You live in a house with four human beings. You deliberately chose to pay for a bare-minimum 20Mbps internet package. Please consult a primary school mathematics textbook.
- Let's do the math: Someone is downloading a 120GB game update, someone else is streaming 4K Netflix, someone is on a Zoom call, and there are six cellphones silently syncing background data. You do not have a "network issue." You have a traffic jam caused by your own cheapness.
- 20 Megabits per second divided by 14 active smart devices equals buffering, suffering, and pixelated misery. The router is not broken; it is crying out for mercy.

*Note: Upgrading your ISP package costs money. Screaming at me that "we should all be able to use it at once" is free, but it defies the laws of physics and will be ignored.*

**BEFORE YOU BUY 15 'SMART' DEVICES FOR YOUR 20MBPS NETWORK...**

- Every single cheap, unbranded smart plug, Wi-Fi lightbulb, and knock-off security camera you connect to that poor, overworked router takes up a slice of the bandwidth.
- Your router was provided for free by your ISP in 2018. It does not have the processing power to simultaneously manage the IP addresses of your entire family's devices plus your Wi-Fi kettle.
- When your internet completely collapses because your smart fridge decided to run a firmware update while you were watching YouTube, that is not a cyberattack. That is a self-inflicted wound.

*Note: I will not configure a custom VLAN and QoS traffic prioritization on a consumer-grade router that feels like it was made of recycled Tupperware. Buy better equipment.*
