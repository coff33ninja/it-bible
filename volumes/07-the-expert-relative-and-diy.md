## The "Expert" Relative & DIY Disasters

---

**BEFORE YOU DEPLOY YOUR 'YOUTUBE EXPERT' RELATIVE...**

- Watching a 15-minute tech vlog does not equal a decade of real-world troubleshooting experience.
- Successfully plugging in an RGB LED strip once does not make someone a network topology architect.
- Your cousin's "system optimizer" downloaded from a shady forum thread is just malware in a trench coat.

*Note: When their "quick 5-minute fix" inevitably bricks the machine, the 'I Told You So' re-imaging fee will be applied to your department.*

---

**BEFORE YOU TELL US WHAT 'YOUR NEPHEW WHO KNOWS COMPUTERS' SAID...**

- Your nephew's YouTube tutorial on running random PowerShell scripts does not apply to our network.
- We will not grant you local admin rights just because a blog post said it would make your system faster.
- We spent weeks configuring those VLANs; do not plug your rogue $15 unmanaged switch into the wall port.

*Note: Unauthorized "Shadow IT" projects will be aggressively dismantled and returned to you in a cardboard box.*

---

**BEFORE YOU FLEX YOUR 'IT DEGREE' ON THE HELP DESK...**

- Your textbook assumed a perfect, sterile environment where users actually read prompts. Welcome to the jungle.
- Certifications are cute, but they don't teach you how to resurrect a server rack that was used as a coffee table.
- Theory is knowing how a VLAN should route. Experience is knowing the user plugged a loopback cable into the wall port because "it looked neat."

*Note: Book smarts will not save you when the unmanaged 10-year-old switch in the ceiling drops the entire subnet.*

---

**BEFORE YOU SAY 'BUT I WATCHED YOU FIX IT LAST TIME'...**

- Watching me run a script does not mean you understand what the script did, why it was necessary, or what dependencies it relied upon.
- A mechanic changing your oil in front of you does not qualify you to rebuild the transmission in your driveway.
- "I tried to do what you did, but now it's a black screen" is the most expensive sentence you can possibly utter in this shop.

*Note: The "I Tried To Fix It Myself" surcharge is automatically applied to any machine that arrives in more pieces than it started with.*

---

**BEFORE YOU BRING ME YOUR 'DIY SMART LIGHTING' PROJECT...**

- I do not care that your ESP32 ARGB zigzag matrix looked great when it was just sitting on a breadboard.
- Splicing a 5V LED strip directly into a generic power supply without a proper standalone controller or a logic level shifter is not 'inventive,' it is an active fire hazard.
- No, I will not re-solder your melted, pocket-sized offline WLED project back together for you. Please learn Ohm's Law.

*Note: Just because your custom circuit fits neatly into a 3D-printed project box does not mean it won't burn your house down.*

---

**BEFORE YOU BRING IN YOUR 'BROKEN' 3D PRINTER...**

- A clogged nozzle on your cheap Creality or Elegoo is a rite of passage, not a critical hardware failure for the helpdesk.
- I manage software deployments and network topography. I am not a mechanic for robotic hot glue guns that move in three dimensions.
- Re-level your bed. Clean your build plate. Dry your filament. Stop bothering me.

*Note: Bringing me an extruder completely encased in a solid, basketball-sized "blob of death" of hardened PETG plastic is grounds for a lifetime ban from this shop.*
