## The Karin Protocol: The Meeting Menace

---

**BEFORE YOU JOIN THE MEETING WITHOUT MUTING YOUR MICROPHONE...**

- "Sorry, can everyone hear me?" — yes, everyone can hear you. Everyone heard you open a chip bag. Everyone heard you sneeze into the abyss. Everyone heard your dog bark at a delivery truck. 
- "One second, let me find the mute button" — you have been in this meeting for 12 minutes. You have been breathing into the mic like a sea lion for 12 minutes. Find it before you join, not after.
- "I think I am on mute" — you are not on mute. You have never been on mute. "I think" is doing a lot of work in that sentence.
- "Can you hear me now?" — I could hear you before. I can hear you now. I will hear you forever.

*Note: The mute button is the most important button in modern business. Learn where it is before you join. Your coworkers do not want to hear your chewing.*

---

**BEFORE YOU SHARE YOUR SCREEN WITHOUT CHECKING WHAT IS VISIBLE...**

- "Oh no, that is my personal email" — that is your personal email. Open. On your screen. With a booking confirmation for a vacation you lied about being "sick" for.
- "Sorry, I did not mean to share that tab" — you shared the tab with your online shopping cart. We all saw the price. We all judged you.
- "Can everyone see my screen?" — everyone can see your screen. Everyone can see the 47 tabs open. Everyone can see the Facebook tab. Everyone can see the dating app notification.
- "Let me just find the document" — you are scrolling through a desktop covered in 200 unorganized files. We are all watching you search. It is uncomfortable for everyone.

*Note: Before you share your screen, close everything you would not want your boss to see. Assume your boss is watching. Because your boss probably is.*

---

**BEFORE YOU JOIN THE MEETING 15 MINUTES LATE WITHOUT CONTEXT...**

- "Sorry I am late, I was in another meeting" — you were in another meeting that ran late because you did not leave on time. Your lack of schedule management is now everyone else's problem.
- "I had a conflict" — you have a calendar. You saw the invitation. You accepted the invitation. The "conflict" existed when you accepted. You chose this.
- "Can someone catch me up?" — I can catch you up in the 15 minutes you missed if you want me to repeat everything. Or you can read the notes that were sent in advance. The notes you did not read.
- "I will watch the recording" — you will not watch the recording. You say you will watch the recording. You never watch the recording. You will ask me tomorrow what we decided.

*Note: Being 15 minutes late to a 30-minute meeting means you missed half of it. That is not "fashionably late." That is "barely present."*

---

**BEFORE YOU ASK A QUESTION THAT WAS ANSWERED ON SLIDE 2...**

- "I have a question" — the answer is on the slide that is still visible. The slide you are looking at. The slide that contains the answer to your question.
- "Sorry, I was multitasking" — you were multitasking. You were not listening. You were checking emails while someone prepared a presentation for you. That is not multitasking. That is being rude.
- "Can you go back a slide?" — I can go back to the slide that answers your question. The slide you did not read. The slide everyone else read.
- "I missed that at the beginning" — you missed it because you joined late. You joined late because you were in another meeting. The chain of causality is clear.

*Note: The first rule of meetings is that the agenda exists for a reason. Read it before you come. Listen while you are here. Ask questions about what was not covered, not what was.*

---

**BEFORE YOU EAT LUNCH DURING A PRESENTATION...**

- "Do not mind me, I am just eating" — everyone minds you. The crunching is coming through the mic. The smacking is coming through the mic. The entire meeting is now about your lunch choices.
- "I did not have time to eat before" — you did not have time to eat before because you were in a meeting that you joined late because you were in another meeting. Your schedule is a house of cards built on poor decisions.
- "It is just a salad" — it is never "just a salad." "Just a salad" is a lie people tell themselves before they spend 20 minutes chewing lettuce into the void.
- "I will be quick" — you will not be quick. Eating takes time. Time you are spending on camera. Time everyone is spending listening to you eat.

*Note: If you would not eat it in a library, do not eat it in a meeting. The camera does not absorb sound. The microphone is not a napkin.*

---

**BEFORE YOU SAY "I THINK WE ARE TALKING ABOUT THE SAME THING" WHEN YOU ARE CLEARLY NOT...**

- "So what I am hearing is..." — you are not hearing. You are projecting. You heard three words and built an entire interpretation that has nothing to do with what was said.
- "Let me just play devil's advocate" — you are not playing devil's advocate. You did not understand the proposal and are now filling the silence with opposition. Ask a question or let us move on.
- "I think we are saying the same thing" — we are not saying the same thing. I said "we need to migrate the server." You said "we should rename the shared drive." These are not the same thing.
- "Just to circle back to an earlier point" — the earlier point was resolved. You missed the resolution because you were multitasking. The circle you are trying to close is already closed.

*Note: If you are confused, say "I am confused." Do not say "I think what you mean is" and then demonstrate that you do not know what anyone means.*

---

**BEFORE YOU VOLUNTEER TO "TAKE ACTION ITEMS" AND THEN DO NOTHING...**

- "I will own this" — you will not own this. You will forget about this before the meeting ends. I will remind you in three days. You will say "oh right" and then forget again.
- "Just send me the notes and I will take care of it" — I will send you the notes. You will file them in a folder called "Action Items 2023" that you have never opened.
- "I can handle that offline" — "offline" is a place where tasks go to die. "Offline" is the grave of good intentions. If you can handle it offline, handle it now.
- "Let me loop in the right people" — you will loop in the right people by forwarding an email with no context. The right people will ignore it because there is no context. The task will evaporate.

*Note: An action item you take but never complete is not an action item. It is a placeholder for disappointment. Either do it or do not volunteer.*
