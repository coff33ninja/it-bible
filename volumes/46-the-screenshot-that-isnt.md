## The Screenshot That Isn't

---

**BEFORE YOU TAKE A PHOTO OF YOUR SCREEN WITH YOUR PHONE...**

- "Can you read this?" — no, we cannot read the glare reflecting your desk lamp off a 2013 monitor at a 37-degree angle.
- "I sent you a screenshot" — you sent a JPEG of a screen taken with another screen. There are pixels on your pixels.
- "You can zoom in, right?" — I can zoom into a photo of a screen showing an error message that is itself a photo of a screen. The resolution loss is exponential.
- "My phone takes better photos than your eyes" — my eyes do not have motion blur. My eyes do not include my thumb in the corner. My eyes do not capture the ceiling fan mid-spin.

**BEFORE YOU SEND A SCREENSHOT OF AN ERROR MESSAGE THAT DOES NOT INCLUDE THE ERROR...**

- "I took a screenshot of it" — you took a screenshot of your desktop wallpaper. The error is gone.
- "It was there a second ago" — correct. And now it is not. And you did not capture it. And I cannot help you.
- "I closed it by accident" — you clicked "Don't show again" and now neither of us will ever see it.

*Note: A screenshot is a digital capture of your display. A photo taken with your phone is a memento of a moment that IT cannot fix.*

---

**BEFORE YOU SEND A SCREENSHOT WITH PERSONAL NOTIFICATIONS VISIBLE...**

- "I did not realize my dating app was open" — you do not realize a lot of things, and now I know all of them.
- "Can you just look at the error and ignore the rest?" — I cannot unsee your shopping cart. I cannot unsee your ex's name.
- "I cropped it before sending" — you cropped it vertically. The Tinder notification is still visible at the top.

*Note: Share your entire screen. Share your entire life. I am not a therapist but I am judging you.*

---

**BEFORE YOU SEND A SCREENSHOT THAT IS ACTUALLY JUST A DESCRIPTION OF WHAT YOU SAW...**

- "It said something about a error" — something. About a error. This is the information you chose to preserve.
- "I did not write it down because I knew I could screenshot it" — you did not screenshot it either.
- "It was a blue screen with white text" — that describes every error screen since 1995. Narrow it down.

*Note: If you describe an error message from memory, you are writing scripture. I need the actual verse.*

---

**BEFORE YOU PHOTOGRAPH YOUR SCREEN WITH ANOTHER SCREEN...**

- "I took a photo of my laptop with my desktop webcam" — you have created an infinite regress of bad decisions.
- "Can you mirror my phone to the TV so I can take a photo of the TV?" — no. Just no.
- "I recorded a video of my screen with my tablet and now I am playing it back on my phone to show you" — you are in too deep. Start over.

*Note: The number of screens involved in your "screenshot" should never exceed one. This is not a Christopher Nolan film.*
