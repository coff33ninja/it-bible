## The Karin Protocol: The Procurement Disaster

---

**BEFORE YOU BUY 12 MONITORS FROM A BRAND NOBODY HAS EVER HEARD OF...**

- "They were really cheap on eBay" — they were really cheap on eBay because they are refurbished units from 2017 with dead pixels and VGA-only inputs. Your docking station does not have VGA. Nobody has VGA.
- "The listing said 'new in box'" — the listing said "new in box" but the box has a shipping label from 2019 and the seal was broken with a knife. The box is new. The monitor is not.
- "I saved the company $200 per monitor" — you saved the company $200 per monitor. You also spent 4 hours trying to make them work. And I spent 2 hours telling you they will not work. And now we have to return them. The savings are gone.
- "They were from a liquidation sale" — liquidation sales exist for a reason. The reason is that the products did not sell. The products did not sell because they are not good. You bought things nobody wanted and now they are our problem.

*Note: Cheap is not the same as cost-effective. Buying something that does not work is always more expensive than buying something that does. Always.*

---

**BEFORE YOU ORDER A LAPTOP WITHOUT IT APPROVAL BECAUSE YOU FOUND A "DEAL"...**

- "I found the same model for $300 less on a different site" — you found the same model on "laptop-deals-2025.net" for $300 less. The site is a scam. The laptop does not exist. The $300 "savings" is now a $1,200 loss.
- "I already ordered it before checking with IT" — you already ordered it. Without checking with IT. The laptop arrived without a charging cable. Without a warranty. Without an OS license. But you saved $50.
- "It is the same specs as the ones IT recommends" — it has the same CPU. It has a different motherboard, different drivers, different BIOS, and a different support path. It is not the same. It is compatible until it is not.
- "I needed it urgently so I just bought it myself" — you needed it urgently because you waited until the last minute. Your urgency is not a reason to bypass the process. The process exists because of people who bypass the process.

*Note: IT-approved hardware is not a suggestion. It is a standard. The standard exists because we have tested that hardware. We know it works. We know how to support it. Your eBay special is not supported.*

---

**BEFORE YOU SUBMIT A REQUEST FOR A $5,000 MACHINE TO "BROWSE THE WEB"...**

- "I need the top-spec model for performance" — you need the top-spec model to check email and open PDFs. You will use 2% of its capability. The remaining 98% will depreciate in your bag.
- "My current laptop is too slow" — your current laptop is too slow because you have 47 browser extensions, 12 startup programs, and a desktop full of files. A new laptop will be slow too. The problem is not the hardware.
- "I need the upgraded graphics card for my work" — your work is spreadsheets. Spreadsheets do not require a dedicated GPU. You want the graphics card because you want to game at lunch. I know. Everyone knows.
- "It is an investment in my productivity" — it is an investment in your ego. The $1,200 model will do everything you need. The $5,000 model will do everything you need plus drain the budget for people who actually need expensive hardware.

*Note: A top-spec machine for a basic user is not an investment. It is waste. The person who needs the powerful machine is the one rendering 3D models, not the one rendering expense reports.*

---

**BEFORE YOU ORDER A SINGLE $3 CABLE FROM AN EXPRESS SHIP OPTION THAT COSTS $25...**

- "I needed it tomorrow" — you needed a cable tomorrow because you lost yours today. The cable costs $3. The shipping costs $25. The total is $28 for a cable that is in the drawer downstairs.
- "Nobody told me where the spare cables are" — I told you where the spare cables are. Last month. When you lost the first cable. You forgot. You lost the cable and the memory of me telling you where the spares are.
- "It is just company money" — it is company money. It is also my budget. The $28 for your cable comes from the same budget that buys the server upgrades. Your impatience is now competing with infrastructure.
- "Can you expense the shipping?" — you can expense the shipping if you want to explain to your manager why you paid $25 to ship a $3 cable that was available for free in the IT supply closet.

*Note: The express shipping fee on a cheap item is a idiot tax. There is a supply closet for a reason. Use it.*

---

**BEFORE YOU BUY SOFTWARE AND SUBMIT THE INVOICE FOR REIMBURSEMENT WITHOUT CHECKING IF WE ALREADY HAVE A LICENSE...**

- "I bought a PDF editor because I needed to edit a PDF" — we have 200 licenses for Adobe Acrobat. You have Adobe Acrobat installed on your computer. You bought a second PDF editor. You now have two PDF editors and one confused expense report.
- "It was only $50 so I did not think it mattered" — $50 does not matter until 200 people each spend $50 on software we already have. Then it is $10,000 of wasted money. Then it matters.
- "I did not know we already had a license" — you did not know because you did not ask. You did not ask because asking is "too much work." The $50 is now the cost of not asking.
- "I will just use my personal account for work" — you will use your personal account for work. Your personal account is not licensed for commercial use. The software company will audit us. The audit will find your personal account.

*Note: Before you buy software, ask IT if we already have it. The answer is usually yes. The savings is usually significant. The process is usually ignored.*

---

**BEFORE YOU ORDER 50 UNITS OF SOMETHING WITHOUT SPEC CHECKING...**

- "They all looked the same in the listing" — they all looked the same in the listing because the listing had one photo for 50 different items. You ordered 50 mice. 30 are wired. 20 are wireless. 15 are left-handed. None are what you need.
- "I did not realize there were different versions" — there are always different versions. That is why there is a model number. You ignored the model number. You ordered by color. You ordered blue. Blue is the wrong version.
- "Can we just use them anyway?" — we can use 7 of them. The rest do not fit, do not connect, or do not work with our systems. 43 units are going to sit in a closet until someone throws them away.
- "I already threw away the boxes" — you already threw away the boxes. The return window is still open. The return policy requires original packaging. You have created 50 units of e-waste.

*Note: A model number is not a suggestion. It is a specification. Read it. Match it. Order it. The extra 30 seconds of reading saves thousands of dollars of waste.*
