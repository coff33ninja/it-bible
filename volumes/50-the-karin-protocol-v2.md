## The Karin Protocol v2: The Sequel

---

**BEFORE YOU REPLY-ALL TO A COMPANY-WIDE EMAIL WITH YOUR PERSONAL REQUEST...**

- "I need a new mouse — thanks" — you replied-all to a 12,000-person distribution list. You now have a new mouse and 11,999 people who know you needed one.
- "Can everyone stop replying-all?" — you replied-all to ask people to stop replying-all. You are now part of the problem you sought to destroy.
- "Please remove me from this thread" — you replied-all to a thread you could have muted in 2 seconds. You have added yourself to more threads.
- "Unsubscribe" — this is not a newsletter. This is your company. You cannot unsubscribe. You work here.

*Note: The reply-all button is a weapon of mass destruction. Treat it like one.*

---

**BEFORE YOU CHANGE YOUR PASSWORD AND THEN FORGET IT WITHIN 30 SECONDS...**

- "I just changed it but now it does not work" — you changed it 17 seconds ago. You have already forgotten it. The password was "Welcome1!" and you have cycled through 47 variations of "Welcome2" through "PasswordResetAgain."
- "It says the password does not meet complexity requirements" — "Password123" is not complex. It is the opposite of complex. It is the default example on every tutorial since 1998.
- "Can I just use my old password?" — you cannot use your old password because you forgot your old password. That is why you reset it. That is the entire reason we are here.
- "I wrote it on a sticky note under my keyboard" — you have created a security vulnerability and a logistical dependency on your own memory. Neither is acceptable.

*Note: A password you need to reset immediately after setting it is not a password. It is a temporary hallucination.*

**BEFORE YOU EMAIL IT DIRECTLY INSTEAD OF USING THE TICKET SYSTEM BECAUSE 'YOU KNOW ME'...**

- "I know you prefer tickets but I thought I would just send you a quick email" — you sent an email to someone who is currently working through a queue of 40 tickets. Your email is not in the queue. Your email is in the void.
- "You can just log it later, right?" — I cannot log it later. Later becomes never. Never becomes a deleted email. Deleted email becomes a problem that still exists.
- "I did not want to bother you with a ticket" — you emailed me directly. That is more bothersome than a ticket. A ticket is self-service. An email is a interruption.
- "I know you are busy so I kept it brief" — "Subject: Help" with no body is not brief. It is a riddle. I do not have time for riddles.

*Note: Emailing me directly because you "know me" does not make us friends. It makes you the person who does not follow the process.*

**BEFORE YOU INSTALL SOFTWARE WITHOUT ASKING AND THEN ACT SURPRISED WHEN IT BREAKS...**

- "I downloaded it from a website" — you downloaded "FreeVideoConverterPro.exe" which installed 3 search toolbars, a new default browser, and a screensaver of animated flames. Your computer now converts video at approximately 0 frames per second.
- "It asked for administrator access so I clicked yes" — it asked for administrator access because it wanted to rewrite your system files. You gave it the keys to the kingdom. It has now changed your homepage to "search.mygoodresults.com."
- "I needed it for work" — you needed "CoolMouseEffects.exe" for work. You process invoices. You do not need cursors that leave rainbow trails.
- "I uninstalled it but the problem is still there" — you uninstalled the toolbar. You did not uninstall the registry changes, the scheduled tasks, the browser extensions, or the certificate it installed. You pulled the weed but left the roots.

*Note: If you install software without IT approval, you are not being resourceful. You are being a vector.*

**BEFORE YOU SAY 'MY COMPUTER IS DOING WEIRD THINGS' AND EXPECT ME TO READ YOUR MIND...**

- "It is acting strange" — "strange" is not a diagnostic category. "Strange" is how I would describe your refusal to elaborate. Be specific or be patient.
- "You know, just weird stuff" — I do not know. I cannot see your screen. I cannot read your mind. "Weird stuff" is meaningless.
- "It is hard to explain" — try. Use words. Use sentences. Use the same language you used to order coffee this morning. You managed to explain a latte. You can explain a popup.
- "I will know it when I see it" — you will not see it because you will click "ok" before I get there. That is how this always goes.

*Note: "Weird stuff" is the IT equivalent of "somewhere over there." Point at the thing. Describe the thing. Let me fix the thing.*

**BEFORE YOU PUT IN A TICKET FOR SOMEONE ELSE WITHOUT TELLING THEM...**

- "I am submitting this on behalf of Bob in sales" — Bob in sales does not know you submitted this. Bob in sales is not expecting my call. Bob in sales is going to be very confused when I ask about his printer.
- "I am helping my colleague who is not technical" — your colleague is technical enough to submit a ticket. You are not helping. You are proxying. Stop proxying.
- "I already told them you would reach out" — you told them I would reach out at a time I did not agree to. You have now made commitments on my calendar without my consent.
- "They are sitting right next to me, can I just put you on speaker?" — no. You cannot put me on speaker. The ticket system is not a conference call.

*Note: Submitting a ticket for someone else without their knowledge is the IT equivalent of a surprise party that nobody wants.*

**BEFORE YOU RESPOND TO AN AUTO-CLOSED TICKET WITH 'THIS IS NOT RESOLVED'...**

- "I just saw the auto-close email and clicked reply" — the auto-close email was sent because the ticket was idle for 14 days. You replying does not reopen it. You have now commented on a closed ticket like a ghost haunting a house that has been sold.
- "I was on vacation" — you were on vacation for 14 days without setting an out-of-office or delegating your active issues. The system assumed you vanished. The system was right.
- "Nobody asked me if it was resolved" — the ticket asked you. Three times. You ignored all three. The system is not a nagging partner. The system has a job to do.
- "I assumed silence meant it was being worked on" — silence meant you were silent. Silence does not mean anything else. The system does not read minds. It reads statuses.

*Note: An auto-closed ticket is not a conversation. It is a funeral. You missed the funeral. The case is buried.*
