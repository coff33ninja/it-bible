## The Karin Protocol: The Printer Warrior

---

**BEFORE YOU YELL AT THE PRINTER LIKE IT IS A SENTIENT BEING THAT HATES YOU...**

- "This printer never works" — this printer works fine for everyone else. It works fine for me. I tested it this morning. The common denominator is not the printer. The common denominator is you.
- "It is possessed" — it is not possessed. The error code is "PC LOAD LETTER." That means the paper tray is loaded with A4 and the printer wants letter. The printer is not demonic. The printer is configured for the wrong paper size.
- "It makes a noise every time I try to print" — the noise is the printer telling you it is out of paper. The manual explains this. The screen explains this. The universe explains this. You are not listening.
- "I hit it and now it works" — you hit it and now it works because you jostled a loose connection. You did not fix it. You delayed the inevitable. The printer will die. When it does, your violence will be remembered.

*Note: Printers are not sentient. They do not have emotions. They do not have grudges. They have error states. Error states have solutions. Screaming is not a solution.*

---

**BEFORE YOU PUT IN A TICKET THAT JUST SAYS "THE PRINTER IS NOT WORKING"...**

- "The printer is broken" — the printer is broken in what way? Is it jammed? Is it offline? Is it out of ink? Is it on fire? "Broken" is not a diagnosis. "Broken" is a state of being.
- "It just stopped working" — things do not "just" stop working. There is a cause. The cause is probably paper. The cause is probably a jam. The cause is never "just because."
- "I tried everything" — you tried nothing. You looked at it. You said "it is not working." You opened a ticket. That is not "everything." That is the minimum.
- "Can you come take a look?" — I can come take a look. But I already know what I will find. I will find a paper jam. I will clear it. I will be done in 30 seconds. The 30 seconds are not the problem. The walk to your desk is the problem.

*Note: "Printer not working" is the most useless ticket in existence. It tells me nothing. It helps nobody. It wastes everyone's time. Include the error message. Include the blinking light pattern. Include something useful.*

---

**BEFORE YOU IGNORE THE BLINKING LIGHT THAT HAS BEEN BLINKING FOR THREE WEEKS...**

- "That light has been on for a while but it still prints" — it still prints because it is using backup ink. The light means the ink is almost empty. When the ink runs out, it will stop printing. The light is a warning. Warnings precede failures.
- "I did not know what the light meant" — you did not know what the light meant because you did not read the manual. The manual explains the lights. The manual is taped to the printer. The manual is in your hand.
- "I assumed someone else would deal with it" — someone else will deal with it when the printer stops working entirely. That someone else is me. That time is now. Congratulations. Your silence created an emergency.
- "It has been blinking for weeks and now it won't print at all" — it has been blinking for weeks because it needed maintenance. You ignored it. Now it needs repair. The repair costs more than the maintenance would have. Your avoidance is expensive.

*Note: A blinking light is not a suggestion. It is a request for attention. Ignoring it does not make it go away. It makes the problem worse. The printer is patient. The printer will win.*

---

**BEFORE YOU LOAD PAPER INCORRECTLY AND THEN BLAME THE PRINTER...**

- "I put paper in and now it is jammed" — you put 50 sheets of paper in upside down, backwards, and folded at the corner. The paper is not loaded. The paper is offended.
- "I used the paper from my desk" — you used paper that has been sitting on your desk for two years. The paper is curled. The paper is damp. The paper has coffee stains. The printer cannot print on a coffee stain.
- "I refilled the tray with the wrong size" — you refilled the legal tray with letter paper. The printer is configured for legal. The paper does not match the setting. The jam is not a jam. It is a size mismatch.
- "I just pulled the paper out when it jammed" — you pulled the paper out. You did not open the access panel. You did not remove the torn pieces. There are now pieces of paper inside the printer that I have to extract with tweezers.

*Note: Loading paper is not a mysterious art. The tray has guides. The guides have markings. The markings tell you which size goes where. Follow them. It is not a test of character.*

---

**BEFORE YOU INSTALL THE WRONG TONER CARTRIDGE AND FORCE IT IN...**

- "It looked like it would fit" — it looked like it would fit because cartridges are vaguely rectangular. The keying pins are different. The chip is different. The color is wrong. "Looked like it would fit" is not a specification.
- "I had to really push to get it in" — you had to push because it is the wrong cartridge. The "push" was the sound of plastic tabs breaking. The cartridge is now stuck. The printer is now broken.
- "I bought a generic brand to save money" — you bought a generic brand that costs 20% less and damages the printer 300% more. The savings on toner will be spent on repairs. The repair budget is now your toner budget.
- "The third-party one was half the price" — the third-party one was half the price because it uses low-quality powder that clogs the drum. The drum is now clogged. The drum replacement costs more than the OEM toner would have.

*Note: Printer toner is not a commodity. The OEM cartridge is expensive for a reason. The cheap cartridge is cheap for a reason. You are not outsmarting the system. You are breaking the printer.*

---

**BEFORE YOU PRINT 200 PAGES AND THEN SAY "I DID NOT MEAN TO PRINT ALL OF THAT"...**

- "I thought it would only print the first page" — you thought clicking "Print" on a 200-page document would only print the first page. That is not how printing works. That has never been how printing works.
- "I did not check the page range" — you did not check the page range because checking the page range takes 3 seconds. You are now standing at the printer watching 200 pages come out. That will take much longer than 3 seconds.
- "Can you cancel it?" — I can cancel it. But 150 pages have already printed. The paper is gone. The toner is gone. The tree is gone. The cancel button is a formality at this point.
- "Just put the paper back in the tray" — you cannot put printed paper back in the tray. That is not how recycling works. That is not how printing works. That is not how any of this works.

*Note: Every print job is a commitment. Check the page count. Check the preview. Check your sanity. Then click Print. Not before.*
