## The "It Worked Yesterday" Expert

---

**BEFORE YOU SAY "IT WORKED YESTERDAY" WITHOUT ANY OTHER INFORMATION...**

- "It worked yesterday" — everything worked yesterday. Yesterday is not today. What changed?
- "Nothing changed" — you installed software. You uninstalled software. You moved files. You changed settings. You "updated" something. You did *something*.
- "I literally did nothing" — you opened your laptop. That is something. Start from there.
- "It worked this morning" — it is now 4 PM. That is 7 hours of unspecified activity.

*Note: "It worked yesterday" is not a diagnostic clue. It is a confession that you have no idea what you did.*

---

**BEFORE YOU SAY "I DID NOT CHANGE ANYTHING" FOR THE FOURTH TIME...**

- "I did not change anything" — you changed your password. You said "maybe my password expired" which means something changed your password. That was you.
- "I swear nothing changed" — your desktop icons are sorted by date. Your browser history is empty. Your wallpaper is different. You changed things.
- "Well I did not change anything intentionally" — intention does not matter. The computer does not care about intent. It cares about state.

*Note: The number of times a user says "nothing changed" is inversely proportional to the number of things that actually changed.*

---

**BEFORE YOU BLAME "AN UPDATE" WITHOUT KNOWING WHICH ONE...**

- "Windows updated and now it is broken" — Windows updates every Tuesday. You are telling me this on Thursday. That is 48 hours of blissful ignorance.
- "I think it was an update" — you think. You do not know. The update log knows. You have never opened the update log.
- "It was probably a driver update" — you do not know what a driver is. You overheard someone say "driver" once and now it is your entire thesis.

*Note: "An update" is not a diagnosis. "An update" is the IT equivalent of "a guy did a thing somewhere."*

---

**BEFORE YOU PROVIDE THE EXACT SAME INFORMATION IN A THIRD TICKET...**

- "I already told you it worked yesterday" — yes, and I asked what changed. You said nothing. That is not helpful.
- "I am just repeating myself because you are not listening" — I am listening. You are not answering.
- "Can you just fix it?" — I can fix it. But first I need to know what broke it. And you broke it. And you do not remember how.

*Note: Repeating the same incomplete information does not make it more complete. It makes it louder.*

---

**BEFORE YOU DEMAND A ROLLBACK TO "YESTERDAY"...**

- "Just roll me back to yesterday" — yesterday is not a backup point. Yesterday is a concept. I cannot restore a concept.
- "Can you undo whatever changed?" — I can undo the specific change if you tell me what it was. You do not know what it was.
- "Just reinstall Windows" — you want me to reinstall Windows because something "worked yesterday" and now it does not. That is like demolishing your house because the toaster broke.

*Note: "Roll back to yesterday" is not a technical request. It is a philosophical position on the nature of time and regret.*
