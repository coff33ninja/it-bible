## The Karin Protocol: The WFH Horror

---

**BEFORE YOU CALL IT ABOUT A VPN ISSUE FROM A COUNTRY YOU DID NOT DISCLOSE YOU WERE IN...**

- "I am working from a coffee shop in Thailand" — you are working from a coffee shop in Thailand without telling anyone. The VPN is not working because the VPN is detecting a login from Bangkok and flagging it as compromised.
- "I did not think it mattered where I was" — it matters. The VPN certificate is region-restricted. The security policy blocks logins from outside the country. You did not think. That is the problem.
- "I am on vacation but I told my boss I would be available" — you told your boss you would be available. You did not tell IT you would be logging in from a hotel in a different hemisphere. IT is not your boss. IT is the gatekeeper.
- "Can you just whitelist my IP?" — you are asking me to whitelist an IP address in a country we do not do business in, from a coffee shop that probably has a keylogger on every terminal. No. I cannot whitelist that.

*Note: If you log in from a foreign country without warning, you will be locked out. That is not a technical glitch. That is a security feature. One that exists because of people exactly like you.*

---

**BEFORE YOU JOIN A VIDEO CALL FROM YOUR BED...**

- "Sorry about the background, I am working from home today" — your background is a pillow. You are in bed. The meeting is about Q4 earnings. You are in bed.
- "My camera is off because my connection is bad" — your connection is fine. You are in bed. I can see the profile picture of your pillow fort.
- "I did not have time to get dressed this morning" — you have been working from home for three years. You have not had time to get dressed in three years. The novelty has worn off. Put a shirt on.
- "I am working from my phone today" — you are working from your phone. In bed. At 11 AM. The phone is not the problem. The bed is the problem. Get out of it.

*Note: Working from home does not mean working from your bed. Your bed is for sleeping. Your desk is for working. The distinction exists for a reason.*

---

**BEFORE YOU CLAIM YOU "CANNOT WORK" BECAUSE OF A MINOR INTERNET ISSUE...**

- "The internet is down" — your internet is down because you did not pay the bill. The bill is in your name. You are responsible for it. I cannot fix your unpaid Comcast account.
- "Wi-Fi is not working" — Wi-Fi is not working because your router is unplugged. You unplugged it to charge your phone. You forgot to plug it back in. The router is in your hand right now.
- "My VPN keeps disconnecting" — your VPN keeps disconnecting because you are on public Wi-Fi on a train. The train is moving through tunnels. The tunnels block signals. The VPN is not the problem. Physics is the problem.
- "I cannot access the shared drive" — you cannot access the shared drive because you are not connected to the VPN. You need the VPN to access the shared drive. You know this. You have been told this. You keep asking.

*Note: The internet is not a utility that IT controls. The internet is a service you pay for. If your home internet is down, IT cannot fix it. IT does not work for Comcast.*

---

**BEFORE YOU TAKE A MEETING FROM YOUR CAR...**

- "I am just pulling into a parking lot" — you are driving. You are on a video call. While driving. I can see the road moving behind you. I can see the traffic.
- "I will just do audio only" — you will just do audio only because you are driving. You should not be doing audio only either. You should be driving.
- "I am parked" — you are parked in a fire lane. The parking brake is not on. The engine is running. You are not parked. You are stopped. There is a difference.
- "I will be home in 5 minutes" — you will be home in 5 minutes. The meeting started 10 minutes ago. You are late because you scheduled back-to-back meetings without travel time. Again.

*Note: Your car is not a conference room. Your car is not an office. Your car is a vehicle. Drive it. Stop trying to join Zoom from the driver's seat.*

---

**BEFORE YOU BLAME "IT" FOR YOUR HOME NETWORK BEING SLOW...**

- "The VPN is making everything slow" — the VPN is adding 30 milliseconds of latency. Your child is streaming 4K video on the same network. That is adding 300 milliseconds. The VPN is not the problem. Your child is the problem.
- "Ever since I started working from home, my internet has been terrible" — ever since you started working from home, your internet usage has doubled. The internet is not worse. You are using it more.
- "Can IT pay for my internet upgrade?" — IT can pay for your internet upgrade if your contract includes a home internet stipend. Check your contract. Do not call IT to ask. IT does not handle payroll.
- "My neighbor said their internet is fine" — your neighbor has a different provider, a different plan, and a different usage pattern. Your neighbor is not a diagnostic tool. Stop asking your neighbor.

*Note: Your home internet speed is not an IT problem. It is a plan problem. Upgrade your plan. Stop blaming the VPN.*

---

**BEFORE YOU WORK FROM A LOCATION WITH A HURRICANE WARNING AND THEN COMPLAIN ABOUT POWER OUTAGES...**

- "The storm knocked out my power" — there is a hurricane. The power is out everywhere. The office is closed. Why are you trying to work?
- "I need to finish this report by end of day" — you need to finish a report during a natural disaster. The report can wait. The hurricane cannot.
- "Can you remote into my computer and save my files?" — your computer is offline. The power is out. The internet is out. I cannot remote into a computer that does not have electricity.
- "I will just use my phone hotspot" — you will use your phone hotspot during a hurricane. Your phone battery will die. The cell towers will go down. You will be stranded with a dead phone and an unfinished report.

*Note: Natural disasters are not a remote work flexibility opportunity. They are a "stay alive and secure your home" situation. The report can wait. Your safety cannot.*

---

**BEFORE YOU SET YOUR STATUS TO "AVAILABLE" AND THEN DISAPPEAR FOR THREE HOURS...**

- "I was doing laundry" — you were doing laundry during work hours. Your status said "Available." Your laundry is not a work activity. Change your status.
- "I took a quick nap" — a quick nap is 20 minutes. You were offline for 3 hours. That is not a nap. That is a second sleep cycle.
- "I was running errands" — you were running errands during work hours. On the clock. Without telling anyone. Your status said "Available." You were not available.
- "I stepped away from my desk for a minute" — you stepped away from your desk for 2 hours and 47 minutes. That is not "a minute." That is "most of the morning."

*Note: Working from home is a privilege. Abusing it is how privileges get revoked. Every time you disappear for hours with your status set to "Available," you make it worse for everyone who actually works from home responsibly.*
