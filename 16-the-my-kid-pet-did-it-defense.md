## The "My Kid/Pet Did It" Defense

---

**BEFORE YOU BLAME THE CAT FOR YOUR DEAD HARD DRIVE...**

- I have heard "my cat walked across the keyboard" approximately 400 times. I have yet to meet a cat that can navigate to diskpart and type "clean."
- Pets shed fur. They do not execute PowerShell scripts, delete system32, or reconfigure your network adapter.
- If your cat genuinely caused the damage, there will be visible evidence. Not a cleanly formatted drive with all your "important business files" mysteriously erased.

*Note: Blaming the pet voids the pet's innocence and insults our intelligence simultaneously.*

---

**BEFORE YOU CLAIM YOUR TODDLER 'DELETED THE COMPANY FILES'...**

- Your two-year-old may be advanced, but not advanced enough to navigate to the server share, authenticate with your saved credentials, and perform a selective deletion of only the critical quarterly reports.
- Children bang on keyboards. They produce gibberish. They do not produce a neatly emptied recycle bin.
- If your child is genuinely that skilled, put them through college and stop wasting their talent on destroying your department budget.

*Note: "My kid did it" is the new "the dog ate my homework." Except this time it is R50,000 worth of forensic data recovery.*

---

**BEFORE YOU SAY YOUR PET 'CHEWED THROUGH THE CABLE'...**

- Yes, pets chew cables. But they do not chew through fibre optic patch cables, HDMI cables, and a power cord simultaneously while leaving the Ethernet cable perfectly intact.
- The tooth marks on your power brick are suspiciously uniform.
- If your pet did chew through it, why did you wait three months to report it?

*Note: Lying about pet damage is a crime against both IT and animal-kind. The cat does not deserve this slander.*

---

**BEFORE YOU BLAME THE CLEANING STAFF...**

- The cleaning staff vacuums around your desk. They do not unplug the server, wipe your hard drive, or steal your mouse.
- "The cleaner must have bumped it" is the go-to excuse for every mysterious hardware failure since the invention of the office.
- If your machine was fine before the cleaner arrived, the most likely explanation is they unplugged a cable and plugged it into the wrong port. That is still your fault for not labelling your cables.

*Note: Stop scapegoating the cleaning staff. They are not your personal IT scapegoats.*

---

**BEFORE YOU CLAIM 'A BUG CRAWLED INTO MY LAPTOP'...**

- Insects are small. They do not type denial-of-service attacks into your command prompt.
- A bug in the case will cause a hardware short if you are extremely unlucky. It will not cause your Facebook account to send spam messages.
- "There must be a virus" — yes, the virus is you clicking "Allow Notifications" on every sketchy website you visit.

*Note: Actual bugs cause hardware failure. Digital bugs cause user-error failure. Know the difference.*
