## Support Etiquette, Remote Sessions & Client Horror

---

**BEFORE YOU CALL FOR 'JUST A QUICK QUESTION'...**

- "Just a quick question" is almost never quick, and it is rarely just a question.
- If the answer requires me remotely logging into your server, running diagnostics, and untangling a database, it is not a question; it is a "Billable Consultation."
- Ambushing a technician on their lunch break or sending a WhatsApp at 9 PM does not bypass the invoicing system.

*Note: Quick questions will be answered with exclusively quick, unhelpful answers. (e.g., "Is the network broken?" "Yes.")*

---

**BEFORE YOU FIGHT ME FOR THE MOUSE DURING A REMOTE SUPPORT SESSION...**

- When your cursor starts moving on its own after you called me for help, that is me.
- Take your hand off the mouse. Stop touching the trackpad.
- Every time you aggressively try to close the command prompt I just opened, you add ten minutes to your resolution time.

*Note: This is a remote troubleshooting session, not a co-op video game. I do not need a player two.*

---

**BEFORE YOU WALK AWAY DURING A REMOTE SUPPORT SESSION...**

- Do not accept the remote connection prompt and then immediately close the laptop lid to "go to lunch."
- My connection to your machine is not telepathy; it requires the device to actually be powered on and awake.
- Staring at a "Target Offline" error on my tablet while you email me from your phone complaining that the issue isn't fixed yet is a special kind of psychological torture.

*Note: Abandoning your machine mid-troubleshooting means your ticket will be routed into an automated workflow from which it may never return.*

---

**BEFORE WE START YOUR REMOTE SUPPORT SESSION...**

- When I ask you to download the remote support tool, do not reply with "I'm not good with computers." That is literally the entire reason you are paying me.
- Do not schedule a remote session and then drive to the grocery store. I cannot troubleshoot your device while it sits completely alone on your kitchen counter.
- Yes, I need the 9-digit connection code. No, the code is not "password."

*Note: Staring blankly at the screen while I repeat "Please click Allow" for the fourth time will result in the session timing out, along with my patience.*

---

**BEFORE YOU QUESTION MY REMOTE SUPPORT SETUP...**

- Yes, I am fixing your infrastructure from a custom Android launcher on my Galaxy Tab. No, that does not mean I am 'playing games' instead of working.
- I spent hours optimizing my entire mobile SSH workflow specifically so I can rescue your business while I am not even at a desk.
- Please do not critique my tools when your primary network admin password is on a sticky note taped to the monitor.

*Note: If you insist on waiting until I am 'at a real computer' to let me fix it, your ticket automatically goes to the back of the queue.*

---

**BEFORE YOU ASK FOR 'LIFETIME FREE SUPPORT'...**

- Replacing your hard drive five years ago does not mean I am now your personal, unpaid digital butler for the rest of eternity.
- I have been doing this for over a decade. I know what natural hardware wear-and-tear looks like, and I know what 'I dropped it in the driveway' looks like.
- My warranty covers the parts I installed, not the malware you intentionally bypassed Chrome's warnings to download.

*Note: "But you're the last one who touched it" is a legally non-binding statement that will be met with an immediate dial tone.*

---

**BEFORE YOU TELL ME 'IT SHOULD BE A FIVE MINUTE JOB'...**

- If it was a five-minute job, you would have done it yourself.
- We both know that 'just looking at the Wi-Fi' is actually going to uncover a nested web of unauthorized switches, IP conflicts, and a rogue DHCP server someone plugged in for fun.
- Do not dictate the timeline of a technical diagnosis before I have even opened the terminal.

*Note: Every time you say "It should be an easy fix," an additional emotional damage surcharge is silently added to the final invoice.*

---

**BEFORE YOU ARGUE ABOUT THE INVOICE...**

- You are not paying R800 for the single line of PowerShell code I typed.
- You are paying for the years of suffering it took for me to know exactly which line of code would fix your registry without vaporizing your operating system.
- My diagnosis fee is non-negotiable. "But you didn't even replace any parts!" is not a valid counter-argument when I just spent two hours undoing your nephew's handiwork.

*Note: Refusal to pay the "Stupidity Tax" means your device will be held hostage as a paperweight until the balance is cleared.*

---

**BEFORE YOU DECLARE A 'BUSINESS-CRITICAL EMERGENCY'...**

- If the issue has been happening for six months, it is not an emergency just because you finally got annoyed by it at 4:55 PM on a Friday.
- Screaming "I am losing thousands of Rands a minute!" does not make the Windows update progress bar move any faster.
- A genuine drop-everything emergency response requires an emergency invoice.

*Note: Curiously, 90% of 'critical emergencies' immediately downgrade to 'we can look at it next week' the exact second the emergency billing rate is mentioned.*

---

**BEFORE YOU THREATEN ME WITH A 'BAD REVIEW'...**

- Go ahead. But know that I contribute heavily to the Local Guides program, I understand exactly how the review algorithms work, and your unhinged 1-star rant will be flagged and ghosted before I even finish my coffee.
- Leaving a bad review because you are mad you had to pay standard customs clearance and import taxes on a sketchy parcel you shipped from overseas is peak delusion.
- I control the network, the backups, and the firewall. A Google Maps review is the absolute least of your worries.

*Note: Extortion is not a valid troubleshooting step.*
