## Thoughts, Prayers & Spiritual IT Delusions

---

**BEFORE YOU RELY ON 'THOUGHTS AND PRAYERS' TO FIX YOUR HARDWARE...**

- God is not a network administrator. He will not assign you a static IP address just because you asked nicely.
- Holy water immediately and permanently voids the manufacturer's warranty. Do not sprinkle things on the motherboard.
- If your IT infrastructure relies entirely on "a wing and a prayer," you belong in a monastery, not a server room.

*Note: When the Blue Screen of Death appears, no amount of spiritual bargaining will recover that 40-page unsaved spreadsheet.*

---

**BEFORE YOU TELL ME 'HOPEFULLY IT WORKS THIS TIME'...**

- "Hope" is not a recognized troubleshooting methodology. I do not deal in hope; I deal in Ping, TraceRt, and error logs.
- Hope is what you have to use when you haven't tested your server backups since 2019.
- I didn't spend a decade staring at terminal windows, reading hex codes, and rebuilding networks to just "hope" a script executes properly.

*Note: If our strategy is officially reduced to hoping, my standard hourly rate transitions into a 'Miracle Worker' retainer, which is significantly more expensive.*

---

**BEFORE YOU QUESTION THE DIAGNOSIS BECAUSE YOU 'HAVE A GOOD FEELING'...**

- Your intuition did not pay for my experience, the countless hours spent fixing broken arrays, or the caffeine required to survive this industry.
- My diagnostic report is not an opening offer for negotiation. It is a forensic autopsy of your poor technological decisions.
- Telling me "let's just wait and see if it fixes itself" is the digital equivalent of ignoring a check engine light while driving headfirst into the Karoo.

*Note: When your "good feeling" inevitably ends in total system failure, the "I Told You So" emergency response fee will be aggressively applied.*

---

**BEFORE YOU ATTEMPT A DIGITAL EXORCISM ON YOUR WORKSTATION...**

- Your machine is not possessed by a demon; it is possessed by 47 malicious browser extensions you downloaded to get free coupon codes.
- Smudging sage in the server room does not cleanse the network of bad vibes; it triggers the fire suppression system and destroys half a million Rands worth of switches.
- Screaming at the monitor in tongues will not fix the corrupted registry, but it will make HR open a file on you.

*Note: Unless the printer actually starts bleeding ink and speaking Aramaic, do not declare a spiritual emergency. Just submit a standard ticket.*

---

**BEFORE YOU TELL ME 'IT'S A MIRACLE' THAT I FIXED IT...**

- It was not a miracle. It was three hours of grueling, meticulous command-line surgery while you were at lunch.
- Calling it a miracle implies that it just magically happened, completely undermining the sheer force of will it took to undo the digital knot you tied.
- Miracles are free. My time, expertise, and refusal to let a machine defeat me are strictly billable.

*Note: Praise is appreciated, but paying the invoice on time is the highest form of worship an IT Technician can receive.*

---

**BEFORE YOU CLAIM 'THE UNIVERSE IS AGAINST YOU'...**

- Computers are literally just refined sand and metal that we tricked into thinking by trapping lightning inside them. They are physically incapable of malice or holding a grudge against you.
- The universe is not targeting your specific workstation. You are just aggressively clicking a flashing banner that says "Download Free RAM."
- Mercury being in retrograde has absolutely zero effect on your local area network's packet loss.

*Note: Karma does not cause catastrophic hardware failure; user error does.*

---

**BEFORE YOU USE YOUR 'LIFE EXPERIENCE' AS A CREDENTIAL...**

- Being 60 years old and "knowing how the real world works" does not grant you an innate, mystical understanding of how a Linux Ubuntu server mounts a Docker volume.
- I respect your eldership, but your decades of general life experience do not override my actual, hands-on IT trauma from rebuilding corrupted arrays since 2013.
- "Back in my day we didn't have these problems" is correct, because back in your day you used a typewriter that didn't require a constant ping to a cloud server to verify its license key.

*Note: General wisdom comes with age. Knowledge of the Windows Print Spooler comes strictly from unrelenting suffering.*

---

**BEFORE YOU START YOUR 'DIGITAL CONFESSION'...**

- I am an IT Technician, not a priest. I do not care about your moral purity, and I am not here to judge the depths of your soul.
- I just need you to admit exactly which sketchy hyperlink you clicked so I can isolate the payload before it hits the company server.
- However, if you look me in the eye, swear on a holy text that you "didn't spill anything," and I open the chassis to find a sticky puddle of dried Oros... we are going to have a serious problem.

*Note: Lying under oath in the IT room instantly voids your warranty and doubles the diagnostic fee.*
