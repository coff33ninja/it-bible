- "The lights were blinking in a pattern I had not seen before" — that pattern was a firmware update in progress. You interrupted a firmware update. The switch is now bricked. It will need to be replaced.

*Note: The reset button is not a "fix the weird lights" button. It is a "factory reset" button. Do not press buttons you do not understand.*
