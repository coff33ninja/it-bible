## Passwords, Accounts & Digital Identity

---

**BEFORE YOU EXPLAIN HOW YOUR PASSWORD 'WAS JUST WORKING YESTERDAY'...**

- Your "complex" password is not "P@$$w0rd!".
- Yes, caps lock counts. No, we cannot "just disable it."
- Your memory of changing it last week is not evidence.

*Note: Repeated attempts using "password123" will result in a mandatory security lecture (with PowerPoint).*

---

**BEFORE YOU ASK WHY GOOGLE 'DOESN'T KNOW IT'S YOU'...**

- You changed your phone, your number, your recovery email, and your location.
- To the automated security system, you look exactly like a cybercriminal wearing your own skin.
- "I forgot my password" does not exempt you from basic account recovery setup.

*Note: We cannot magically bypass corporate security protocols just because you are "in a hurry."*

---

**BEFORE YOU ASK ME TO 'BYPASS THE OTP'...**

- The One-Time PIN is literally the only thing standing between your life savings and a teenager with a laptop on the other side of the world.
- No, I cannot "turn it off because it's annoying."
- If your phone number changed and you didn't tell the bank, and now you can't receive the SMS to authorize your payment, that is a 'You' problem. I cannot hack the cellular tower to reroute your messages.

*Note: Any request to intentionally bypass two-factor authentication on a financial platform will be met with uproarious, mocking laughter until you leave the premises.*

---

**BEFORE YOU ASK ME TO 'JUST OPEN YOUR GOOGLE DOCS' TO FIX A TYPO...**

- I am an IT Technician, not an omnipotent digital deity. I do not have native, backdoor access to your private Google account.
- I cannot bypass your two-factor authentication, and I cannot "just peek" at your documents without your active session and credentials.
- Stop assuming I am sitting at my desk reading your private files. I have an entire homelab of Docker containers to maintain; your data is not that interesting.

*Note: If you refuse to type in your own password during a remote session because "I should already have it," your ticket is instantly closed.*

---

**BEFORE YOU BLAME THE 'BANKING APP UPDATE' FOR YOUR EMPTY ACCOUNT...**

- Software updates patch security vulnerabilities and add features; they do not steal your money.
- If your account is empty, it is because you clicked a link in an SMS that said "URGENT: YOUR PARCEL IS DELAYED" and gleefully typed your credit card details into a website hosted in a basement three continents away.
- Uninstalling and reinstalling the banking app will not magically reverse a wire transfer.

*Note: Telling me "but the website looked so real" does not change the fact that the URL was www.fnb-secure-login-totally-real-update.biz.*
