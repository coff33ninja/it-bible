## Liquid Damage & Physical Destruction

---

**BEFORE YOU GIVE YOUR LAPTOP A DRINK...**

- Your laptop is not thirsty. It does not want your coffee, your tea, your water, or your caramel macchiato.
- We can smell the sticky residue. We will find the corroded motherboard traces. We will know exactly what you did.
- "It just stopped working" is not a valid description when the insides look like a crime scene from a CSI episode.

*Note: The diagnostic fee remains the same whether you admit to the spill or force us to find the evidence ourselves.*

---

**BEFORE YOU TRY TO INVENT A 'NEW' EXCUSE FOR THE DAMAGE...**

- I have been doing this for over a decade. I promise you, I have seen every conceivable way a human being can destroy a motherboard.
- Your "unprecedented" hardware failure is a textbook liquid spill.
- Please save us both the theatrical performance.

*Note: Lying about how it broke means I have to spend extra time acting surprised when I find out exactly how you broke it. You will be billed for my acting.*

---

**BEFORE YOU BLAME 'DIVINE INTERVENTION' FOR PHYSICAL DAMAGE...**

- Lightning striking the local substation and frying your ungrounded motherboard because you refused to buy a R200 surge protector is not a mysterious Act of God. It is a highly predictable Act of Stupidity.
- Deities do not randomly descend from the heavens to shatter the LCD screen of your tablet from the center outward. You dropped it in the driveway.
- Blaming a higher power for your refusal to safely eject a USB drive before yanking it out is theological malpractice.
- If you bring me a laptop that looks like it was run over by a bakkie and claim "divine intervention," I am going to charge you an exorcism fee.

*Note: Holy water immediately and permanently voids the manufacturer's warranty. Do not sprinkle things on the motherboard.*

---

**BEFORE YOU BRING IN A DEVICE WITH 'VITALLY IMPORTANT FILES'...**

- If the files were truly important, they would exist in more than one physical location.
- Handing me a clicking, smoking hard drive and saying, "My entire business is on there, don't lose it," is not a backup strategy. It is a tragedy.
- I am an IT Technician. I do not have a sterile data-recovery cleanroom in my back pocket.

*Note: Presenting you with a quote for R15,000 professional platter-recovery services usually cures your sudden attachment to those "important files" immediately.*

---

**BEFORE YOU BLAME 'EVER SINCE YOU FIXED MY PRINTER'...**

- Fixing your paper jam last Tuesday did not cause your motherboard to short-circuit today.
- Installing an antivirus does not make you forget your online banking password.
- Causation does not equal correlation, especially when your timeline involves you dropping the machine on a tile floor in the interim.

*Note: The "Ever Since" warranty only covers the space-time continuum directly related to the original repair, not your subsequent poor life choices.*

---

**BEFORE YOU DEMAND SUPPORT FOR YOUR 'BARGAIN' HARDWARE...**

- Buying the absolute cheapest, no-name tablet or knock-off accessory online does not make you a financial genius; it makes you my problem.
- There are no drivers. There is no manual. The manufacturer's website is a parked domain from 2015.
- No, I cannot "just write a script to make it work." I am an IT Technician, not a necromancer.

*Note: If the device feels suspiciously hollow and rattles when you shake it, do not bring it to this desk.*

---

**BEFORE YOU BRING IN MYSTERY PARTS YOU BOUGHT ON CLEARANCE...**

- We provide professional IT services, we are not a Lego assembly service for the cheapest knock-off components you managed to find scrolling Takealot at 2 AM.
- If you bring me an unbranded motherboard, RAM that smells like ozone, and a power supply held together by sheer hope, I will not guarantee it will boot.
- "Can't you just make it fit?" is a question for a carpenter.

*Note: Providing your own sketchy hardware doubles the diagnostic fee and completely voids any sympathy when the machine inevitably catches fire.*

---

**BEFORE YOU BRING IN THAT 'AMAZING DEAL' YOU BOUGHT ON SALE...**

- That absolute bottom-tier laptop you grabbed off Takealot or Incredible Connection for next to nothing is basically a glorified digital photo frame with a keyboard.
- You cannot run heavy accounting software, render 4K video, or host your local databases on a Celeron processor with eMMC storage.
- No, I cannot "just make it faster." You bought a toaster, and now you are mad it cannot fly.

*Note: I cannot download more RAM for you. Please accept the consequences of your financial decisions.*
