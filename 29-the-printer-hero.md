## The Printer Hero

---

**BEFORE YOU PRINT 200 PAGES AND TAKE ONLY 1...**

- You just printed 200 full-colour pages, walked to the printer, grabbed the single page you actually needed, and left the remaining 199 pages in the tray. This is not an accident. You do this every time.
- The remaining pages sit in the output tray until someone else either throws them away, adds them to the growing pile of orphaned print jobs, or takes them home by mistake.
- Every page you abandon costs the company money. Toner is not cheap. Paper is not free. The environment is not a fan of your habits either.

*Note: The print queue is not a "selective harvesting system." If you print it, take it. All of it.*

---

**BEFORE YOU PRINT IN COLOUR 'BECAUSE IT LOOKS NICER'...**

- That internal memo did not need to be printed in colour. The spreadsheet did not need colour. The 50-page training manual definitely did not need colour.
- Colour toner costs 5 times more than black toner. Printing 200 pages of internal documentation in full colour is not "making it look nice." It is burning the office supply budget.
- "But the charts have different coloured lines" — the lines are labelled. The legend exists. Black and white is sufficient. You are not designing a brochure.

*Note: Colour printing is for client-facing materials and approved marketing collateral. Your Monday morning status report does not qualify.*

---

**BEFORE YOU PRINT THE ENTIRE INTERNET 'JUST IN CASE'...**

- Printing a 300-page PDF, a 150-page research report, and an entire website "to read later" — you will not read it later. You will leave it on the printer until someone throws it away.
- "I like to have a hard copy" — then print it, bind it, and file it. Do not print it and let it accumulate in a pile on top of the printer that nobody touches for 6 months.
- The printer is not a publishing house. It is a utilitarian device for occasional document production, not a Gutenberg press for your digital hoarding.

*Note: Paperless was supposed to reduce printing. You interpreted it as a challenge to print more before it is too late.*

---

**BEFORE YOU WALK AWAY FROM THE PRINTER WHILE IT IS STILL PRINTING...**

- The printer is chugging through a 50-page document. You pressed Print, turned around, and walked back to your desk. You will return in 20 minutes and the pages will have been taken by someone else.
- "I will come back in a few minutes" — those few minutes turn into an hour, then the next day, then never. Your orphaned print job becomes a permanent resident of the output tray.
- Corporate printers are not restaurant kitchens. They do not call out your order number when it is ready. Stand there for 47 seconds and collect your pages.

*Note: The walk of shame back to an empty printer tray with no pages and a growing pile of someone else's abandoned documents is self-inflicted.*

---

**BEFORE YOU LOAD PAPER INCORRECTLY AND BLAME THE PRINTER...**

- The paper tray has a diagram. The diagram shows which way the paper faces, which side is up, and how to align the edges. You ignored the diagram and now the paper is jammed at a 30-degree angle.
- There is a label inside the tray that says "This side up." You put it "this side down." The printer does not know which way is up. It just tries to feed what you gave it.
- You loaded 15 sheets of paper loosely on top of a half-empty ream and expected the feeder to handle the height difference gracefully. It did not.

*Note: Paper loading is not a guessing game. Follow the diagram. The diagram exists because someone before you failed at this exact task.*

---

**BEFORE YOU CALL IT BECAUSE THE PRINTER 'IS NOT PRINTING'...**

- Is the printer on? Check the power light. Do not call me before checking the power light.
- Is there paper in the tray? Look at the tray. Open it. Check. Do not call me before checking the paper tray.
- Is the toner empty? The printer will tell you on its screen. Look at the screen. Read the message. Do not call me before reading the message on the screen.

*Note: The printer has an LCD display. It shows error messages. Read the error message before calling IT. The message "Paper Jam" means there is a paper jam. It does not mean "call IT immediately."*
