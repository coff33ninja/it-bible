## AI Hallucinations & Modern Tech Nightmares

---

**BEFORE YOU PASTE THAT AI-GENERATED SCRIPT INTO POWERSHELL...**

- A language model is essentially a highly confident autocomplete. It does not know our network topology, it does not understand our firewall rules, and it certainly does not care if it vaporizes the active directory.
- If the AI hallucinates a command and tells you to delete a core registry key to "optimize performance," and you blindly hit 'Enter', you are not a programmer. You are a digital liability.
- Unless you actually know how to code, or at the very least have an n8n workflow safely archiving your technical prompts and responses into Notion so we can audit the disaster, step away from the terminal.

*Note: "But the chatbot said it was perfectly safe" is a defense that will be laughed out of the server room.*

---

**BEFORE YOU SUBMIT A TICKET FOR THE 'CUSTOM APP' THE AI BUILT YOU...**

- Prompting a web interface to "build a billing system in Python" and receiving 400 lines of disjointed, hallucinated spaghetti code does not make you a software developer.
- The AI just invented three libraries that do not exist in this universe and forgot to include a database connection.
- I will not debug your digital alphabet soup. If you aren't spinning up a local LLM via Ollama to actually test and isolate your own code in a controlled environment, do not bring your compilation errors to my desk.

*Note: My hourly rate triples the exact second I have to read code written by a robot that forgot how math works.*

---

**BEFORE YOU PANIC THAT 'AI HACKERS' CLONED YOUR IDENTITY...**

- You are not a high-level corporate executive, and no international cyber syndicate is spending computing power to train a voice cloning model on your Microsoft Teams meetings.
- Yes, voice cloning technology exists — it is great for passion projects like making an Arlecchino voice model for a gaming mod — but the scammer who called you is just playing a generic MP3 recording of a person coughing. They did not synthesize your voice.
- Stop clicking on Facebook ads for "Free Crypto," and your identity will magically remain secure.

*Note: The biggest threat to your personal data is not an artificial superintelligence; it is your own inability to recognize a phishing email.*

---

**BEFORE YOU USE AI TO 'SUMMARIZE' COMPANY DOCUMENTS...**

- Pasting highly confidential, proprietary client financial data into a public web chatbot so you don't have to read a 10-page PDF is a catastrophic breach of security.
- You are actively training an external corporation's language model on our private company secrets.
- Furthermore, the AI just hallucinated an entire paragraph about a merger that isn't happening because it got confused by a similar article from 2018. Now you look like an idiot in the board meeting.

*Note: Using a language model to do your reading comprehension for you will result in your immediate transfer to a department that primarily uses crayons.*

---

**BEFORE YOU DEMAND I FIX THE AI'S 'FACTUAL ERROR'...**

- I am an IT Technician. I manage the physical hardware and the local network. I am not the lead developer at OpenAI, Google, or Anthropic.
- If the chatbot confidently tells you that the capital of France is "Cheese," I cannot "go into the system and correct it." I do not control the multi-billion-parameter neural network housed in a server farm in California.
- Stop submitting helpdesk tickets because a language model lost an argument with you.

*Note: Arguing with an algorithm is a symptom of madness. Forcing your IT department to participate in that argument is grounds for termination.*

---

**BEFORE YOU ASK ME TO 'PUT AI' ON YOUR OFFICE MACHINE...**

- Running a local Large Language Model via Ollama requires actual, dedicated hardware, not a six-year-old Celeron processor gasping for air.
- "AI" is not magic. It cannot summarize your spreadsheets if your machine doesn't even have the memory to open the spreadsheet in the first place.
- Also, your device's voice assistant is not "listening to your thoughts" or "hearing" you like a human. It receives processed text converted from your mumbling. Speak clearly.

*Note: Asking for 'the ChatGPT' to be installed on your network router will result in me staring at you in profound, unblinking silence.*
