## WFH / VPN Horror

---

**BEFORE YOU COMPLAIN THAT THE VPN IS 'TOO SLOW'...**

- The VPN encrypts all your traffic and routes it through the corporate network. That overhead costs about 10-15% of your bandwidth. That is not a bug. That is the price of security.
- If you are connecting from a coffee shop with 5Mbps upload speed and complaining that the VPN is slow, the VPN is not the problem. Your decision to work from a location with dial-up speeds is the problem.
- Streaming 4K YouTube while on the VPN and blaming the VPN for your laggy Teams calls is rich. The VPN is not the bottleneck. Your lack of bandwidth management is.

*Note: The VPN does not make your internet slower. Your internet makes your VPN slower. Check your home connection before blaming the tunnel.*

---

**BEFORE YOU WORK FROM A HAMMOCK AND BLAME TECH...**

- Working from a hammock looks idyllic on Instagram. In reality, your posture is destroyed, your laptop is overheating on your stomach, and your Wi-Fi signal is going through your body at a weird angle.
- "My neck hurts from looking down at my screen" — that is ergonomics, not IT. I cannot prescribe you a chiropractor.
- "My laptop keeps overheating" — it is on a soft fabric surface with no airflow. Of course it is overheating. Put it on a desk like a normal person.

*Note: Working from a hammock, a bean bag, or your bed is not a technical problem. It is a life choice with consequences. Live with them.*

---

**BEFORE YOU DEMAND A SECOND MONITOR FOR YOUR 'HOME OFFICE'...**

- The company issued you a laptop, not a home studio. If you want a multi-monitor setup for your spare bedroom, the company may provide it — but you have to ask properly, not demand it.
- "I cannot work without two monitors" — you can. Millions of people do. You just do not want to. There is a difference.
- If you are working from a kitchen counter with your laptop next to a toaster, a second monitor will not fix your workspace. A proper desk will.

*Note: WFH equipment requests require a business justification, not a preference. "I want" is not a justification.*

---

**BEFORE YOU JOIN A VIDEO CALL FROM A NOISY LOCATION...**

- Your microphone is picking up the coffee grinder, your neighbour's barking dog, and the construction site outside your window. Everyone on the call can hear it. They are all muted and judging you.
- "Sorry, can you repeat that?" — I would love to, but I cannot hear you over the sound of your own blender.
- Mute your microphone when you are not speaking. It is the single most important rule of video conferencing. Everything else is secondary.

*Note: Joining a call from a public space with no headset is not "flexible working." It is "hostile audio." Invest in a microphone or invest in silence.*

---

**BEFORE YOU CLAIM YOUR WEBCAM 'DOES NOT WORK'...**

- There is a sticker over the camera lens. I can see it. You put it there for "privacy." Now you are complaining that the camera does not show your face.
- A physical privacy shutter is not a defect. It is a feature. Slide it open and the camera will work exactly as designed.
- "The camera is black" — because the lens cap is on. Remove the lens cap. I should not have to explain this.

*Note: Webcams do not spontaneously stop working. They are covered, disabled, or disconnected. Check all three before calling IT.*

---

**BEFORE YOU ASK IT TO FIX YOUR HOME WI-FI...**

- Your home network is not part of the corporate infrastructure. I do not manage your Telkom router, your guests stealing your bandwidth, or your placement of the router behind a metal cabinet.
- "The internet is slow" — have you tried rebooting your router? No? Then why are you calling me?
- If you want a corporate-grade home network, the company may provide one. If you are using the free router your ISP gave you in 2016, you get what you pay for.

*Note: WFH support covers the company laptop and the VPN. It does not cover your decision to put the router in the cupboard under the stairs.*

---

**BEFORE YOU LOG INTO THE VPN FROM A COMPROMISED PERSONAL DEVICE...**

- Your personal laptop has no antivirus, no firewall, and no IT oversight. You use it to download torrents, visit sketchy sites, and click every "Your Flash Player is Outdated" ad that appears.
- Connecting that device to the corporate VPN is like inviting a stranger with a contagious disease into a hospital ward.
- "But I needed to check my email quickly" — install the company-approved email app on your phone. Do not bridge your cesspool of a personal laptop into the corporate network.

*Note: The VPN is a privileged access tunnel. Treat it like one. Do not let unsecured devices through the front door.*
