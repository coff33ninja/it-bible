## The Chair Whisperer

---

**BEFORE YOU BLAME IT FOR YOUR BROKEN OFFICE CHAIR...**

- I manage servers, networks, and software deployments. I do not manage pneumatic gas cylinders, castor wheels, or lumbar support adjustments.
- "The chair won't stay up" — that is a gas lift mechanism. It is mechanical, not digital. I cannot reboot your chair.
- "It makes a clicking noise when I lean back" — stop leaning back at a 45-degree angle with your feet on the desk. Chairs are not rocking horses.

*Note: IT stands for Information Technology, not Interior Technology. Call facilities or procurement. Leave us out of your ergonomic crisis.*

---

**BEFORE YOU CLAIM YOUR CHAIR 'JUST BROKE'...**

- Chairs do not "just break." They break because you weigh 120kg and you sit on the edge of the cushion with all your weight on the front rim, or you spin violently in it like a carnival ride.
- The same person who breaks a chair every 6 months and blames "poor quality" is not unlucky. They are a repeating pattern with a common variable.
- "It was fine yesterday" — and a car with worn brake pads was "fine yesterday" until it was not. Wear and tear is a thing. You are not the victim of a chair conspiracy.

*Note: If you have broken 4 chairs in 3 years, the problem is not the chairs. The problem is how you interact with chairs.*

---

**BEFORE YOU ASK IT TO FIX THE CHAIR ARMEST...**

- The armrest is held on by bolts. Bolts require a hex key, not a software patch. I do not carry a hex key. I carry a screwdriver for opening server chassis.
- "Can you just order me a new chair?" — I can order you a new docking station. I cannot order you a new throne. Submit a procurement request.
- The chair is not a peripheral device. It does not connect via USB. It does not need drivers. It is a chair. Sit in it properly and it will last.

*Note: Our support contract covers laptops, monitors, and docking stations. It does not cover the thing you sit on while using them.*

---

**BEFORE YOU SPIN IN YOUR CHAIR AND CLAIM IT IS 'STUCK'...**

- The chair is not stuck. You spun it 15 times in one direction for fun and now the tension in the gas cylinder is twisted. Untwist it by spinning the other way the same number of times.
- Office chairs are not amusement park rides. They are ergonomic tools. Treat them with the respect they do not deserve.
- "It is leaning to one side" — because you sit with your legs crossed and all your weight on one hip every single day for 8 hours. The foam has memory. It remembers your bad posture.

*Note: A chair that leans to one side is not broken. It is a mirror reflecting your asymmetrical sitting habits.*
